# Hackathon report: Prompt interference

Paper: *Herding AI Cats* (2023)

**Question:** Do pacing and humor instructions interfere when combined in a modern Gemini model?

**Method:** Compare four prompt conditions on identical recipe tasks; record model, date, number of calls, and prompts.

**Results:** Fill in actual pacing compliance and humor success for each condition. Include failures and successes.

**Extension:** Separate the instructions into labeled sections and compare to combined instructions.

**Limitations:** Short test cases, small sample, manual labeling, and differences from the original CarlaBot study.
